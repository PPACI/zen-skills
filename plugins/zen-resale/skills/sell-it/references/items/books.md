# Books

## Listing

- Fields: title, author, volume or part, edition, language, ISBN, condition.
- Vinted requires an ISBN to publish a book. Get it from a photo of the back cover or copyright page; when splitting a lot, ask the user for those photos or numbers before starting, and check each one with Vinted's ISBN lookup.
- Read title, author, and volume from the cover photo. An ISBN prefix names the publisher, not the author.
- Title: the book's title and author as buyers type them. A lot's title names its books ("<Title A>, <Title B>, <Title C> – lot 3 livres"), not only a theme ("lot livres business").
- Vinted: category "Books & Media" › "Books" › genre; the ISBN fills "Author", "Book title", and "Language" on the edit page. "Medium" parcel fits most single books.

## Pricing

- Search each book by title and author, then by title and volume for series.
- A single sought-after volume can sell for more than the whole lot it sits in.
- Lots of unrelated books (two subjects that share no buyer) get views but no sales; split them.
