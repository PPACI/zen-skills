# Electronics and small appliances

## Before writing

- Ask whether it works, and for a photo powered on when that shows it (backlight, screen). A powered-on photo answers "does it work?" before buyers ask.
- Ask which accessories come with it (remote, cable, power supply, box). A remote can be stored on the device itself and pass for a part of it.

## Listing

- Identify the exact model from the photo (logo, printed text, key icons), not only the brand.
- Keyboards: identify the layout from the keycaps and name it in the title. A layout other than the local one is the first thing buyers need to know. Swiss QWERTZ keycaps carry both German and French letters (ü/è, ö/é, ä/à), § top left and ç on 4; German QWERTZ has only Ü, Ö, Ä and ^ top left. The user may guess the wrong one.
- Do not state what the user cannot vouch for (filter age, battery health); leave it out rather than guess.

## Pricing

- Check sold and active listings of the exact model on the target marketplace. Variants (mini, TKL, V2) sell at different prices.
- An accessory sold alone (a remote) signals how much it adds to the price.
- A non-local keyboard layout narrows the buyers: price at the low end of the range.
